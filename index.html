How the tax is computed

New regime, FY 2026-27 (unchanged in Budget 2026). Simplified — check Form 16 or a CA for filing.

Slabs

0–4L nil, 4–8L 5%, 8–12L 10%, 12–16L 15%, 16–20L 20%, 20–24L 25%, above 24L 30%, plus 4% cess. Standard deduction ₹75,000 for salaried income.

Rebate & marginal relief

Section 87A rebate of ₹60,000 makes taxable income up to ₹12 lakh effectively tax-free — about ₹12.75 lakh of salary. Just above that, marginal relief caps the tax at the income exceeding ₹12 lakh; this tool applies it, so there is no cliff at the boundary.

What's skipped

Old regime (HRA, 80C, home-loan interest), surcharge above ₹50 lakh, and employer-specific exemptions. Special-rate income (capital gains) is not modelled. PF defaults to 12% of an assumed 50%-of-CTC basic — overwrite it from your payslip.
